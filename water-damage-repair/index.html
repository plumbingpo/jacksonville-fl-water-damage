A dry house is not yet a finished house. After readings return to normal, the bare subfloor, cut-out drywall and missing floors still need attention. Water damage repair is the rebuild. For Jacksonville homeowners, our crew installs replacements for anything unsalvageable and blends them with the existing finishes until the space stops looking like a job site.

The scope of repairs varies a lot. A minor loss can need just a couple of feet of wallboard plus baseboard and a coat of paint. On a large one it can mean insulation, full sheets of drywall, subfloor, hardwood or laminate, cabinet boxes, trim and doors. Our aim is a like-for-like replacement, and when a true match cannot be found we say so directly and let you decide.

We do not close anything in while it is still damp. Ahead of installing anything new, our team meters the framing and subfloor to verify normal moisture. Cover wood that is still damp, and mold grows unseen behind the new paint. You also receive a written scope and schedule ahead of the work, which spells out what gets rebuilt and when.

How We Handle Water Damage Repair

Our systematic approach delivers quality results on every project. Here's what you can expect when you choose Jacksonville Restoration Co:

1

Confirm It Is Dry

We take final moisture readings on framing, subfloor and remaining drywall. Reconstruction only begins once those numbers are at normal levels for the material, so nothing damp gets sealed in.

2

Put the Scope in Writing

We hand you a list, room by room, covering what gets replaced, planned materials and the likely schedule. When there is an open claim, the scope is coordinated with your adjuster.

3

Rebuild the Structure

We refill open cavities with insulation, hang, tape and finish drywall, and repair the subfloor. After that we install trim, cabinetry and floors, ordering the work so completed surfaces are not damaged.

4

Final Finishes and Walkthrough

We match texture, prime and paint, and reinstall doors, baseboards and hardware. Then we walk every room with you and fix anything on the list before we call the job complete.

When to Call for Water Damage Repair in Jacksonville

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • The subfloor gives or feels spongy underfoot where water once stood.
  • A leak overhead left the ceiling drywall cracked, sagging or falling apart.
  • Tiles sound hollow, have come loose or show cracked grout since water got beneath them.
  • After drying, the hardwood is still cupped, crowned or lifting.
  • Since the water damage, certain doors drag on the jamb or miss the latch.
  • Where water ran down a wall, the paint has blistered, peeled or turned brown.
  • During drying, sections of wallboard came out and the framing is still exposed.

Maintenance Tips for Water Damage Repair

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Before a contractor closes a floor or wall, request the final moisture numbers.
  • Do not paint or patch over a water stain until the source is fixed.
  • File the written scope and photos of the completed repair with your household papers.
  • Keep leftover flooring, tile and paint details so future repairs can be matched.
  • Laundry rooms, bathrooms and kitchens should be rebuilt with moisture resistant wallboard and proper backer board.
  • Run the bath fan during showers and for a while afterward so rebuilt walls stay dry.

Why Water Damage Repair Is Not a DIY Job

This is not the same as a standard renovation project. Whoever rebuilds has to understand where the water travelled, what it affected and whether the framing inside the wall has fully dried. We build from the moisture records made during drying, which tell us the studs that got wet, the subfloor that was treated and the spots needing a second look. Knowing the background stops issues from being hidden behind new wallboard and a coat of paint.

Matching is the other skill. You will see a visible patch every single day. Wall texture is blended, paint is feathered out to natural breaks, and flooring is sourced to line up with the boards already there when available. If nothing matches, you get options from us, for example new flooring run to a doorway. Our team pulls any permits the job needs and sends updates to your insurance company.
