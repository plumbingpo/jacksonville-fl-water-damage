Of all the problems an owner can have, sewage coming up through the drains ranks among the most dangerous. Anyone who comes in contact with contaminated material is exposed to parasites, bacteria and viruses, among them giardia, E. coli, salmonella and hepatitis, all carried in raw sewage and all dangerous. Jacksonville Restoration Co provides emergency sewage cleanup throughout Jacksonville, responding 24/7 with properly trained technicians, appropriate personal protective equipment and EPA-registered disinfectants to decontaminate your property safely.

The industry classifies sewage as black water, or Category 3, which is the most dangerous and most contaminated type of water damage there is. Because of it, cleanup follows the toughest rules in the trade, which means full protective gear, barriers to contain the area, tearing out any porous material that met sewage and disinfecting all that is left. For Category 3 water damage the reference is IICRC S500, and our crews follow it to bring the building back to safe occupancy.

The cleanup process involves removing all standing sewage, extracting contaminated water from structural materials, removing and properly disposing of every porous material that contacted sewage (carpet, padding, drywall, insulation), thoroughly disinfecting hard surfaces, applying antimicrobial treatments and completing structural drying to prevent secondary mold growth. We also deodorize the affected area with industrial-grade hydroxyl generators and thermal fogging equipment to eliminate lingering sewage odors.

Why Hire a Professional for Sewage Cleanup?

Sewage cleanup is not something any homeowner should attempt. The biological hazards in raw sewage require specialized training, proper personal protective equipment (Tyvek suits, respirators, protective gloves and boots) and EPA-registered disinfection products that are not available to consumers. Hepatitis, gastrointestinal disease and infections of the skin or respiratory system are among the serious illnesses these pathogens cause.

Professional sewage cleanup also requires proper disposal of contaminated materials as biohazard waste. It is illegal to throw sewage-contaminated insulation, drywall or carpet in the regular trash, and doing so puts the community and the workers who collect it at risk. A certified restoration company follows proper disposal protocols and works with licensed facilities that accept biohazard waste.

How to Prevent Sewage Cleanup Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Know the location and condition of your sewer cleanout for emergency access
  • Keep downspouts and sump pump discharge out of the sanitary sewer so it does not overload in heavy rain
  • Protect against municipal sewer backups by having a backwater valve fitted where your line connects
  • Hair and other debris are easy to stop with simple strainers on tub, shower and sink drains
  • Every 2 to 3 years, get a sewer camera run through the line to spot developing issues
  • Never pour grease, oil or food waste down your drains, since these cause blockages

How Our Sewage Cleanup Works

We follow a proven process to ensure every job is completed to the highest standards. Here's how we handle sewage cleanup from start to finish:

1

Hazard Check and Containment

First the crew identifies hazards, determines how much of the property the sewage has contaminated and puts containment in place to protect rooms that were not affected.

2

Sewage Removal & Material Disposal

All standing sewage is extracted. Contaminated porous materials including carpet, padding, drywall and insulation are removed and properly disposed of as biohazard waste.

3

Decontamination & Sanitization

EPA-registered antimicrobials are used to disinfect what is left after a thorough cleaning of each surface. Airborne contaminants and pathogens are captured by HEPA air scrubbers.

4

Drying & Odor Elimination

Residual moisture is taken out with commercial-grade dryers. Industrial deodorization eliminates sewage odors. Finally, a clearance test verifies that people can safely use the area again.

Signs You Need Sewage Cleanup in Jacksonville

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Toilets or drains that gurgle whenever you run water somewhere else
  • Every sink, tub and shower draining slowly even after you plunge them
  • Sewage or dark water backing up through floor drains, toilets or tubs
  • Multiple drains in your home backing up at the same time
  • Lower levels or drain openings giving off a heavy sewage odor, sometimes like rotten eggs
  • Sewage residue visible around drain openings or on basement floors
  • Wet spots on floors or walls accompanied by foul odors
