Typical Water Damage Problems in Palm Bay

Every house has a few weak points where water tends to get loose. The list below covers what we are asked to clean up most for Palm Bay owners.

Water heaters: Sediment collects in the bottom of a tank and slowly eats through the steel. Look for moisture at the base, rust streaks on the jacket or a small pool that keeps coming back. A split tank does not stop at one tankful, because the inlet keeps feeding it until the valve is shut. Heaters in a hallway closet or attic can ruin flooring and ceilings well beyond the utility area.

Roofs: One strong storm is enough to peel back shingles and push water beneath the roof covering. Skylights, plumbing vents and chimneys are other entry points once their seals crack with age. Since water follows the framing downhill, the ceiling stain rarely sits directly below the actual opening. On Palm Bay roof leak calls, our crew removes saturated insulation, sets drying equipment and can cover the opening until a roofer arrives.

Freezing: Depending on the season, a hard overnight freeze can burst lines that were fine the day before. Outdoor spigots, sprinkler feeds and pipes in crawl spaces or along outside walls are hit first. Vacant homes and rooms with the heat turned down are especially exposed. If a faucet only trickles on a freezing morning, shut off the water and call before the pipe thaws.

Backups: A drain that gurgles or several fixtures that slow down together point to a blockage in the main line. When the clog becomes complete, every flush and every load of laundry comes back out downstairs. The water is unsanitary, and hard surfaces need disinfecting after the solids and liquid are removed. Until a plumber opens the line, do not use sinks or toilets and keep everyone clear of the mess.

Seepage: Dampness at the base of a basement or foundation wall often means water is collecting outside. Short downspouts, soil that slopes toward the house and blocked footing drains are frequent causes. White chalky deposits, peeling masonry paint and a persistent damp smell are early warnings. We remove any standing water in Palm Bay homes, dry the walls and slab, and explain what likely caused it.

How Palm Bay Homeowners Can Avoid Water Damage
